How to move

In TTMath on Mars, drag to look around and walk with W A S D or the arrow keys; on a phone, drag to look and use the MOVE stick at the bottom left. In Jim's house, drag the whole house to turn it and click a label to open that part; drag a 360° view to look round and click a ring on the floor to walk on.

What's real

In TTMath on Mars, the ground around the starting point, about 260 × 260 m, is NASA JPL's 3D model built from Curiosity rover photos. Everything else in the demos is imagined, including demo 2; the maps in its design plan use real NASA imagery. The science pages are real, with sources.

Terrain: NASA-AMMOS 3DTilesSampleData (msl-dingo-gap). Imagery: NASA/JPL-Caltech/MSSS, NASA/JPL-Caltech, NASA/JPL-Caltech/University of Arizona, USGS Astrogeology. Science: NASA, JPL, ESA and the studies listed on each science page. Rendered with Three.js.
