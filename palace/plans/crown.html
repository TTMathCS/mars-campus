The Crown for the day, 41 m above the plain

Light, views and company, for about six hours a day: the rooms are placed by the sun, so there is one for every hour of the sol. Morning in the east (breakfast, the bedroom up), midday in the south (the salon, the pool), evening in the west (dining, the sunset lounge), the north light for the library and the studio, and the night sky in the north-east.
