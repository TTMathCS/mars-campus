Floor plans Rev G for Jim's review

Every room designed before any more pictures are drawn: what it is for, what else it can be used for, where it is and how big, and a code to refer to it. Two of a kind only where the two have different jobs. 179 rooms and areas.

Why G: one set of letters runs through the whole design. Rev A and Rev B were floor plans (B approved on 30 Sep 2026), Rev C to E the design book, Rev F the Orb's rooms, still a question for Jim.

How the house is planned

  • Day up, night down. The Crown is the day house: light, views and company, about six hours a day. The Pentagon is home for the evenings and the nights, under 16 m of soil, where everyone sleeps, Jim and his guests.
  • A room for every hour. The Crown's rooms are placed by the sun: breakfast in the east, the salon and the pool in the south, dinner and the sunset in the west, the library and the studio in the north light, the stars in the north-east.
  • Jim's home is one sector. On L1, sector 1 holds everything he uses every day, close together: music room, family room, dining room, study, bedroom, bath, kitchen.
  • The rest by use: the club, the baths, the library and the guests on L1; gardens on L2; making and running the house on L3; life support on L4; arrivals on L5.

How the codes work

  • L1-02: the Pentagon, level 1, room 02. Numbered level by level, sector by sector, from the atrium outwards.
  • C-10: the Crown, room 10, numbered clockwise from the Arrival part; a spire's upper floor follows its part.
  • O-07: the Orb; O-00 is the Wormhole Gate. G-01: the ground.
  • Shared areas have letters: L1-AT atrium terrace, L1-PC portal column, L1-ST streets, L1-AV avenues, CC1 to CC5 corner cores, C-GL the Glide.
