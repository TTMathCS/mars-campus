Arcadia design plan · 02 · 1

The Crown, room by room

The Crown's main floor is a ring 20 m wide inside and 276 m across, 41 m above the Stone Garden. Its rooms are 16.5 m deep and 12.5 m tall, 20 m in the halls under its five spires. The Glide, a moving walkway, runs along the garden side and links every part in a 734 m loop. Windows 2.5 m tall at eye level run through both walls, so walking along the ring one sees out on both sides: the plain on one, the Stone Garden and the mirror Orb on the other. The five spires rise over the Arrival hall, the Salon, the Dining hall, the Library and the Observatory, and each holds a portal.

C-01 to C-05 · part 1 · east · a spire

Arrival

Coming home. The pod flies into the hangar from the garden side; the hangar fills with air in about 90 seconds while the dust is blown off; then the Door, a ring of light 5 m across, opens for Jim and his guests alone. The Arrival hall beyond is 20 m tall under its spire: the great maple in its round banquette under a halo of light, the windows looking back over the garden to the Orb.

RoomsC-01 Suit room · C-02 Pod hangar · C-03 The Door · C-04 Arrival hall; C-05 Dock control upstairs
Made ofPolished basalt underfoot, regolith plaster, a wall of basalt round the Door, a round basalt planter, bronze reveals round the windows, a wall of bronze and glass onto the Glide.
In itThe great tree, a Japanese maple 7 m tall, red turning orange, in a round banquette of cognac leather under a halo of light 9 m across; leather benches by the Door; agaves along the windows; Van Gogh's Starry Night on the end wall, and the portal to the Orb, the other spires and the Pentagon between two kentia palms.
C-06 to C-08 · part 2 · south-east

The master suite up

Jim's rooms for the evening up in the Crown: to dress for dinner, to rest after it and read in the grand bed under the stars before he goes down to sleep. The bed faces the south-east windows, where the sun rises straight across the room. It is closed to the Glide by a wall of bronze and switchable glass, frosted for privacy, with the suite's own doors.

RoomsC-06 Dressing room · C-07 Bedroom up · C-08 Bath up, with a soaking tub by the windows

DoorsA pair of walnut pivot doors 4 m tall from the Glide into the dressing room, lit by alabaster pendants; open doorways from the dressing room to the bedroom and from the bedroom to the bath.
The bed2.4 m wide, against a headboard wall 6.4 m wide in walnut and channelled velvet, lit from behind; walnut nightstands with marble tops float from it; alabaster pendants hang to either side, and a halo of light 3.8 m across overhead.
Seats and tablesA boucle sofa 4.6 m long and two moss velvet club chairs round a travertine table; two rust velvet club chairs at the windows in front of the bed; a writing desk of walnut, leather and bronze with a leather executive chair.
PlantsTwo red Japanese maples, 3.4 m, in basalt planters either side of the bed; a kentia palm; a fiddle-leaf fig by the desk; white and magenta orchids.
Made ofPale oak walls, a floor of linen-coloured stone, an oak-slat ceiling; bronze, walnut, velvet, travertine.

C-20 to C-22 · part 6 · west

The sunset lounge

The west side of the ring: low sofas face the west slots, and at sunset the sun shines straight down the room for a few minutes while the sky round it turns blue, as the sky of Mars does. At one end the guests' lounge, where visitors spend their days; at the other a gallery of Jim's paintings and his photographs of Mars.

RoomsC-20 Guests' lounge · C-21 Sunset lounge · C-22 Gallery
In itLow sofas and chairs facing the slots, rugs, olive trees.
