T-06 · The Crescent: the Academy

Eight classrooms named after mathematicians, a study hall, a competition room, a games room and lounge, the teachers' room and washrooms on both floors, in a crescent that wraps the back of the dome. The upper floor opens onto the palace's back terrace; the lower floor onto the garden.
