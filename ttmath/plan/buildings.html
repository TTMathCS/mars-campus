The buildings

Eleven new buildings and spaces, coded T-06 to T-16 after today's T-01 to T-05; click a code for its floor plans and rooms. Heights are checked against the line of sight from the start over the whole footprint: the number after the height is how far the building stays below it at its tightest point.

T-06The Crescent: the Academy
Eight classrooms, each named after a mathematician (Euclid, Archimedes, Hypatia, Fibonacci, Gauss, Noether, Ramanujan, Turing), a study hall, a competition room, a games room and lounge, the teachers' room, on two floors in a crescent that wraps the back of the dome. The upper floor opens onto the palace's back terrace, the lower floor onto the garden.		2,600 m²9 m · 4.1 m below the line of sight
T-07Infinity Hall
A lecture theatre with 240 seats on 12 rows climbing the slope like a Greek theatre, a stage and a screen 14 m wide: talks, competitions, films, the graduation.		900 m²11 m at the stage, 6.5 m at the foyer · 1.8 m below the line of sight
T-08Garden of Primes
Two glass barrel vaults full of real plants under grow lights: vegetables and herbs for the café, citrus and fig trees, a fern grotto, benches among the beds. A biology class and a quiet place to read.		1,010 m²8 m · 15.0 m below the line of sight
T-09Fibonacci Garden
A garden in three terraces down the slope from the palace to the observatory, its paths laid on a golden spiral: basalt gravel, Mars rock, math sculptures (a Klein bottle, a trefoil knot, a Möbius bench), lights at Fibonacci distances.		outdoor0 m · 20.7 m below the line of sight
T-10Observatory
A tower with a telescope dome on top, at the foot of the garden on the palace's axis: Mars's night sky, Earth and the Moon as evening stars, Phobos and Deimos. A viewing deck round the dome.		1,020 m²24 m · 18.6 m below the line of sight
T-11Low-gravity Sports Dome
A sports hall under a dome 34 m across for games in Mars gravity (0.38 g): basketball with 2.6 times the hang time, a climbing wall, a fitness gallery with treadmills that load you to your Earth weight.		900 m²16 m · 18.6 m below the line of sight
T-12Robotics and Rover Hangar
The engineering lab where students build robots and rovers, and the hangar for the campus rovers, with a suitport for each and a workshop. The test yard next to it has a track with ramps and rocks.		750 m²8 m · 32.0 m below the line of sight
T-13Rover test yard
A track over gravel, ramps, a rock garden and a sand trap, for the students' rovers.		outdoor0 m · 31.7 m below the line of sight
T-14Pod Port
A parking field for flying pods, ready for travel when it comes: six landing pads 14 m across with lit rings and charging masts, pods parked on four of them, a taxi lane to the terminal.		outdoor0 m · 55.1 m below the line of sight
T-15Pod terminal
A glass lounge by the pads: check-in, a waiting room with the view, a charging room, the pod crew's office and two boarding collars, so nobody needs a suit.		310 m²6 m · 47.1 m below the line of sight
T-16Sun court
Where the solar field stood behind the classroom wing (the campus is on the city's grid): an outdoor classroom round a bronze armillary sundial that keeps Mars time, stone benches in a half-circle.		outdoor2.8 m · 0.8 m below the line of sight
