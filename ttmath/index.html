TTMath Mars Campus

An imagined TTMath campus on Mars, set at "Dingo Gap" in Gale Crater. The ground around your starting point, about 260 × 260 m, is real: NASA JPL's 3D model built from Curiosity rover photos (around sol 528) and HiRISE orbital images. The campus, the landscape beyond, the vehicles and the sky effects are simulated.

The campus is a dream of TTMath on Mars. White Dune Shells rise from the ground like wind-blown sails, each with a glowing glass face. Behind them a landing ring floats above the ground and glass pods hover on curved sky bridges. Rovers park beside the shells, and a flying craft circles the campus.

Things to try

  • Walk up the ridge ahead: after a few steps the campus comes into view. Walk down to the glowing entrance.
  • Press play and watch the sunset turn to twilight: the campus lights glow, clouds keep shining after the Sun is gone, and stars come out.
  • In twilight, find Earth and the Moon low in the west, and Phobos rising.
  • Jump: in Mars gravity you stay in the air about three times as long.
  • Turn on Sound for the wind and your footsteps.
