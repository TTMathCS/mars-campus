# The Crown, revision H (from 4 Oct 2026)

Jim's asks of 4 Oct 2026, in his words where they matter, and what each became. The Crown comes first ("stay here
focus on improve each image/design and focus on walking on the crown. then generalize the new
requirements/experience and move to other rooms"); then the same for every other room.

## The building
- **Wider** ("reduce the radius of inner circle so there are should be more interior spaces. priority"): the inner
  wall moves from r = 125 m to r = 115 m. The ring is 20 m wide inside (it was 10 m): the Glide's 3.5 m along the
  garden side, then rooms about 16.5 m deep to the outer wall. The Stone Garden below shrinks to r = 102 m.
- **Taller** ("the crown inside is too short. I need increase the ceilling height much higher and look more
  spacious"): the ceiling is 12.5 m above the floor (it was 6 m), rising to 20 m (it was 9.5 m) in the halls under the
  five spires. The roof between the spires is at +56 m; the spires still reach +90 m.
- **Windows at eye level, cleanly made** ("all the windows on the wall looks not straight lines. the border of the
  windows looks broken"; "I need them to be lower so when people walking their height should see through the windows
  on both sides"): through both walls, 0.5 to 3.0 m above the floor, 5 m wide every 8 m. Each is lined in one closed
  piece of bronze, 4 cm thick, standing 2 cm proud of both faces, so its edges run straight and unbroken; a window a
  cross wall would cut is left out, so every window stands whole in its room.
- **Glazed onto the Glide, every room as the master suite** ("all rooms in the crown should have same window as
  master bedroom", 6 Oct): a wall of bronze and glass from the floor to the ceiling on each room's Glide side, clear
  or frosted as the room needs, with pivot doors.

## The rooms
- **Seating sized to the rooms, never small chairs** ("the sofa are so small while the environement space is huge";
  "I hate those small chairs"): long sofas along the walls, deep sectionals and curved sofas round a table, daybeds,
  banquettes, big lounge chairs; groups face their table, not the walls.
- **Plants chosen for each room** ("all the plants so far … are same, and looks strange"): from the plant library
  (`palace/tools/render/plants.py`), each species grown as it grows, in planters sized to it; checked in the studio
  (`specimens.py`) before it goes into a room.
- **Paintings** ("I need paintings on the wall with good design around crown"): public-domain masterpieces and large
  canvases, each in a designed frame with its own light; the Gallery holds the most.
- **The master suite** ("needs a door. plants is not good. bed too small. need fancy bed lights. replace table/chair
  with more luxury and good design ones"; "same thing for all rest rooms"): a door onto the Glide, a grand bed with
  lights of its own, a luxurious desk and seats.
- **The star lounge** (C-30): its outer and inner walls of switchable glass, dark by day at the touch of a switch,
  clear at night for the stars.
- **The sky garden** (C-34): "I like black platform instead of black": its meaning is to be confirmed with Jim.
