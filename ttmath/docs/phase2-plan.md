# Phase 2: the campus three times bigger

Jim, 4 Oct 2026: "expand TTMath Campus, need more buildings and spaces. expand so far to 3 times bigger … I also need
parking lot for flying pod which is future proof for travel"; then "building expansion / new rooms/area design as
priority". Published plan: <https://ttmathcs.github.io/mars-campus/ttmath/plan/>.

## Where the new quarter goes, and why

- **Behind the Math Palace.** The campus must stay hidden from the start point until you walk over the ridge (CP-1).
  In front of the palace the ground hides only 3 to 6 m, so nothing tall fits there. Behind it the ground falls 15 to
  20 m to the plain, out of the line of sight: a building there may be 13 to 55 m tall and still not show.
- **Into the slope.** The Crescent's lower floor, Infinity Hall's rows and the garden's terraces step down with the
  ground, so the buildings sit low against it.
- **On the palace's axis.** A new back door in the rotunda (T09-04) opens onto the back terrace (T09-01); the axis runs
  on through the Crescent's hall, down the garden's steps, to the observatory (T-10) at the foot of the garden.
- **Around a garden.** Infinity Hall (T-07) on one side of the garden, the Garden of Primes (T-08) on the other; the
  sports dome, the hangar and the pod port further out on the plain, where the height limit is generous.
- **No power plant on the campus.** It is on the grid of the city nearby (Jim, 4 Oct 2026: "no need for plant
  generators, since the power is supplied centrally by the city close by. free the space up for other purposes"). The
  solar field behind the classroom wing goes; its place becomes the Sun court (T-16). The rovers lose their roof panels
  and charge at the hangar.

## Sealed: one pressurised campus

Jim, 5 Oct 2026: "since bad conditions, all the schools buildings should be connected and sealed, so there should not
be open area to the air. all open space should be covered by dome or sealed". So:

- **Every open space goes under glass.** The courtyard between the wings under a glass vault that springs from the
  wings' roof edges (T04-01), its crown falling with the line of sight from 10.6 m to 6.2 m; the back terrace as the
  winter garden under a vault that wraps the back of the dome (T09-01); a garden gallery along the Crescent's garden
  front under a sloping glass roof (T06-15); the Fibonacci Garden under three geodesic glass domes stepping down the
  slope (T09-02, -03, -05); the Sun court sunk to the lobby's floor under a low vault (T-16).
- **Every building joined to the next** by a sealed link (T-17): a concrete trough half sunk in the ground and banked
  with regolith, a glass vault on steel ribs above. The palace, the wings, the courtyard hall, the winter garden, the
  Crescent, the domes, Infinity Hall, the Garden of Primes, the observatory, the sports dome, the hangar and the pod
  terminal make one walk in shirt sleeves.
- **Airlocks only where people meet the outside:** the entrance under the gateway (T04-02, two sets of sliding doors and
  a chamber; the visitor from the start comes in here), the hangar's suit room and the rovers' suitports (T-12), the
  pods' boarding collars (T04-03, T15-05). The Crescent's end exits become enclosed emergency stairs (T06-16, -17).
- **Outside stay only the vehicles' ground:** the rover test yard (T-13) and the pod pads (T-14).
- **The observatory moves 26 m down the slope** (rad −172) to stand at the foot of the domes, and the terminal 10 m
  east, so the domes, the observatory and the terminal join in a row.

## Bigger classrooms, higher ceilings

Jim, 5 Oct 2026: "class rooms are all too small and roof are too low. feels depressed". The Crescent grows: 46 to 62 m
from the dome's centre (was 60), −52° to +52° (was ±50°), the floors 5.6 m apart (was 4.2) and the ceilings 4.5 m high
(was 3.0; 3.8 m in the corridors). Six classrooms of about 17 by 12 m (was eight of 11 by 11): Euclid, Hypatia,
Fibonacci and Noether upstairs, Gauss and Turing downstairs; the study hall, the competition room (Ramanujan), the games
room and lounge, the teachers' room, washrooms on both floors. Its roof rises to about 7 m above the palace's floor,
still 4.6 m under the line of sight. In the wings the flat ceilings go as high as the roofs allow (up to about 4.2 m,
with a lower band at the back where the roof comes down).

## The flying pods

Jim, 5 Oct 2026: "add some flying pod I can take and can drive the flying pod to see the eagle view". One pod waits at
the pod stop beside the entrance airlock (T04-03), four on the pod port's pads. Walk up to one to board it and fly it
over the campus and the crater: up to 400 m high, up to 40 m/s; set it down on a pad or on open, level ground and step
out. The camera's near plane moves out as the pod climbs, so the ground and roofs stay crisp from high up.

## Design decisions (and why)

- **Infinity Hall is a hillside theatre**: the stage at the low end, the rows climbing toward the palace. Turned the
  other way, the rows would rise against the falling ground and the hall would stand 13 m out of the slope.
- **The Crescent's corridor is on the dome side**, so every classroom has the garden view; on the lower floor the
  corridor runs along the retaining wall. Washrooms at the same end of both floors, one above the other.
- **The sports dome has a fitness gallery, not a running track**: a track round the edge cannot fit with a court and a
  climbing wall in 34 m, and running in 0.38 g needs harnesses anyway (treadmills that load you to Earth weight).
- **The pods dock to the terminal** through boarding collars, so nobody needs a suit to board.
- **An armillary sundial in the Sun court**, not one drawn on the ground: Gale crater is at 5.4° S, where the flat
  (analemmatic) kind degenerates into a line.
