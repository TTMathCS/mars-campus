Mars facts

The planet as orbiters, landers and rovers have measured it, one subject a page. Mars is half as wide as Earth, with a bit over a third of its gravity, a day 40 minutes longer, air less than a hundredth as thick, and cold and radiation no one could live in unprotected. It also has ice, sunlight, carbon dioxide and soil: what people need to make water, air, fuel and buildings.
