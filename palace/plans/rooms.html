Shared areas on every Pentagon level

  • L1-AT (and L2-AT … L5-AT) Atrium terrace: The terrace along the glass round the atrium, with hanging gardens.
  • L1-BR (and L2-BR … L5-BR) Bridges: The bridges from the terrace to the portal column.
  • L1-PC (and L2-PC … L5-PC) Portal column: Portals to every level, the Crown and the Orb.
  • L1-ST (and L2-ST … L5-ST) Streets: Four streets, 4 m wide, between the rings; lit from above on L1.
  • L1-AV (and L2-AV … L5-AV) Avenues: Five avenues, 5 m wide, from the atrium's corners out to the corner cores.
  • L1-CC (and L2-CC … L5-CC) Corner cores: At the five outer corners: stairs (the fallback for the portals), pressure doors and services.
