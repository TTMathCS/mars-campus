The Pentagon

You came down by portal to L1, 24 m under the Stone Garden. You are on a bridge in the atrium: a five-sided shaft 35 m across and 68 m deep, with the sky lens of the Sun Well at the top and the sun court at the bottom.

Walk the bridges and the terraces of hanging gardens. The portal column in the middle takes you to any level in one step: go down to L2, 16 m tall under a sky of lamps, with its lake, forest, orchard and meadow, or to the sun court on L5.
