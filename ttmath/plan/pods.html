The pod port

A parking field for flying pods (T-14) with its terminal (T-15), at the far end of the campus on the plain, where nothing on the ground can be seen from the start. Built now so the campus is ready for travel by air when it comes.

  • Six pads 14 m across in two rows, dark with white markings, a ring of lights round each, a charging mast beside it; a taxi lane to the terminal.
  • The pods: four parked on the pads, two-seat craft about 6 m long: a teardrop cabin in white composite with a tinted canopy, four big ducted rotors (Mars's thin air needs large blades), landing skids, navigation lights. No toy shapes: proportions and detail of a real aircraft.
  • The terminal: a glass lounge under a thin white roof, check-in, a waiting room with the view of the pads, a charging room.
