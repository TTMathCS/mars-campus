TTMath Mars Campus

An imagined TTMath campus on Mars, set at "Dingo Gap" in Gale Crater. The ground around your starting point, about 260 × 260 m, is real: NASA JPL's 3D model built from Curiosity rover photos (around sol 528) and HiRISE orbital images. The campus, the landscape beyond, the vehicles and the sky effects are simulated.

The campus is a dream of TTMath on Mars. An avenue of white Dune Shells leads to the Math Palace: a glass dome 56 m across whose lattice follows the spirals of a sunflower, 21 ribs winding one way and 34 the other.

Inside, a balcony rings a rotunda sunk 10 m into the ground. Two grand stairs sweep down to a Penrose-tiled floor with a sunflower medallion, twelve lit math exhibits, the five Platonic solids, a floating golden Möbius strip and a 24 m Foucault pendulum. A landing ring floats beside the palace with rovers docked under it, and a flying craft circles the dome.

Things to try

  • Walk up the ridge ahead: after a few steps the campus comes into view. Follow the avenue to the palace door (hold Shift to run).
  • Inside, take the grand stairs down and read the exhibits. Count the spirals in the floor's sunflower and in the dome: they are Fibonacci numbers.
  • The light studs along the avenue sit 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13, 21 and 34 m from the door.
  • Time the pendulum: one swing takes 16 s in Mars gravity, against 9.8 s on Earth.
  • Press play and watch the sunset turn to twilight: the palace lights glow, clouds keep shining after the Sun is gone, and stars come out.
  • In twilight, find Earth and the Moon low in the west, and Phobos rising.
  • Jump: in Mars gravity you stay in the air about three times as long.
  • Turn on Sound for the wind, your footsteps and the echo under the dome.
