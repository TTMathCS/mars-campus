Two of a kind

Jim, 2 Oct 2026: two of a kind is fine "as long as they could be used for multiple purpose". Each pair below has two different jobs. Most pairs follow the house's rule: by day up in the Crown, in the light and the view, for about six hours; by night down in the Pentagon, under 16 m of soil, where everyone sleeps.
