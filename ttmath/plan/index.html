TTMath campus, phase 2

The campus grows to about three times its size: ten new buildings and spaces on the slope behind the Math Palace, where the ground falls 15 to 20 m to the plain. From the start nothing new can be seen: every building stays under the ridge's line of sight, checked against the real terrain. You reach the new quarter through a new back door in the rotunda, or round the dome.

Floor area today: about 3,420 m². New: 6,820 m². Together: 10,240 m², 3.0 times today's.
