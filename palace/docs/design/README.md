here too. Each page links to its full chapter.

All ten chapters are written. Every room is as on the floor plans Rev G, which Jim approved
on 3 Oct 2026, with the Orb 48 m across.

## Real, or future?

Almost everything uses what is known today: the site and its ice, the air and radiation on Mars, making fuel from
ice and air, fission reactors, shielding with soil, rocket flight in thin air, farming under lights,
the 26-month launch windows. A few things are dreams, on purpose, because Jim asked for them. Each is marked
*future technology* in the book, with a real fallback where safety needs one:

| Future technology | Real fallback |
| --- | --- |
| Anti-gravity drives that hold the Crown up | Five pads under the spires; stored energy lowers the Crown onto them |
| Portals instead of lifts | Stairs in the Pentagon's corner cores, robot carriers, the pods and escape capsules |
| The Wormhole Gate, a ball that shoots you like light to any place and time | — (it is the dream) |
| The universe in VR, in 3D in the Orb's rooms, with no glasses | Light VR glasses |
| Radiation glass in the Orb's rest rooms | Deep acrylic and water windows, about 1.5 m thick |
