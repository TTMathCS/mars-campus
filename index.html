Mars Campus

Walkable 3D places on Mars, each one imagined and built to discover on foot, from a campus on real NASA ground in Gale Crater to a palace above Arcadia Planitia. They run in the browser on a computer or a phone.

How to move

On a computer, drag to look around and walk with W A S D or the arrow keys. On a phone, drag to look and use the MOVE stick at the bottom left.

What's real

In TTMath on Mars, the ground around the starting point, about 260 × 260 m, is NASA JPL's 3D model built from Curiosity rover photos. Everything else, including the whole of Arcadia Palace and its landscape, is simulated.

Terrain: NASA-AMMOS 3DTilesSampleData (msl-dingo-gap). Imagery: NASA/JPL-Caltech/MSSS, NASA/JPL-Caltech, NASA/JPL-Caltech/University of Arizona, USGS Astrogeology. Rendered with Three.js.
