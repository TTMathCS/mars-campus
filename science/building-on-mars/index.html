Building on Mars

How people could build and live on Mars with the science and engineering we have, one topic a page. The numbers come from missions, experiments and published studies; the few that are our own estimates say so. Each page ends with how Arcadia, Jim's home, does it.

Three ideas run through all of it

  • Bring little, make the rest. Machines, electronics and medicines come from Earth; water, air, fuel and building materials are made on Mars.
  • Bury it. A few metres of soil stop the radiation, and more hold the air pressure in.
  • Never depend on the Sun alone. A dust storm can hide it for weeks.
