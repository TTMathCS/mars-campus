Chapter 04

Interiors

The rooms of the house, what they look like and what they are made of. The Crown is for daylight, views, music and guests; the Pentagon for sleep, water, gardens and work. Everywhere the aim is the same: calm rooms of real materials that feel like Earth, in a house that could only be on Mars.

Design languageEarth materials, Mars light

The house uses a small palette of real materials, most of them made on Mars: stone and ceramic from the ground, glass from its sand, metals from its rocks, wood from the trees on L2. Surfaces are matt and warm; colour comes from the materials themselves, never from paint.

Light sets the mood. In the Crown it comes through the window slots: soft by day, a blade of gold at sunrise and sunset. In the Pentagon every room has a sky ceiling, an artificial skylight that shows a sun and a blue sky with real optics. Products that do this exist on Earth today. It follows the time of day and can show Mars, Earth or the stars.

Water and plants are in almost every room, for the air, the sound and the smell. Quiet comes free: 3 m walls in the Crown and 16 m of soil over the Pentagon keep out every outside sound.

No room shows its machines. Air comes in through slots along the floor and leaves through the ceiling edge; power, data and water run in the walls; robots use their own service corridors behind the rooms.

Two master suitesOne up for mornings, one down for nights

Jim asked for two master suites, one up and one down. They sit one above the other in the south-east: the Crown's suite in the dip between the Arrival and Salon spires, the Pentagon's in sector 1 of L1, 65 m below.

Master suite up · the Crown

995 m² along the ring. The bed faces the south-east window slot, so on a clear morning the sun rises straight across the room. The walls are lined in pale oak; the floor is linen-coloured stone. The private spa has a soaking pool with the same view. Dressing room and bath are at either end. The portals in the Arrival and Salon spires are a short ride along the Glide.

Master suite down · L1

About 900 m² in ring B, sector 1. The bedroom opens onto a moss garden with a still pool, under a sky ceiling that dims to stars at night and brightens slowly before waking. Under 24 m of ground it is the quietest, safest room on Mars, and where Jim sleeps most nights (chapter 08). The family room and the kitchen are next door; a corner core with a portal is two minutes away.

Coming homeFrom the pod to the Arrival hall

The hangar takes the pod straight in from the garden side. The door closes, and the hangar fills with air in about 90 seconds while fans and brushes blow the dust off the pod. Then the pod's door opens onto a floor of warm stone.

The suit room beside it is for trips outside on foot. Suits hang in ports in the outer wall and never come in: you climb into a suit from inside and it seals behind you, so no Mars dust enters the house. NASA has built and tested suit ports like this for rovers.

The Door is a round opening 5 m across at the back of the hangar, closed by an iris of light. It recognises you by face, eyes and the way you walk, and opens only for Jim and his guests.

The Arrival hall is the first room: 9 m tall, a basalt floor, a long olive-wood bench, and the first window slot, looking back across the garden to the Orb. From here the Glide leads round the ring, and a portal leads down to L1.

Light through the solA day of 24 h 40 min

A Mars day, a sol, lasts 24 hours 40 minutes, so the house keeps Mars time and the sky ceilings follow the real sun. People adapt to the extra 40 minutes easily; Mars rover teams have lived on Mars time for months.

The ceilings follow the pattern our bodies expect: warm light at waking, bright cool light at midday, warm and dim in the evening, then darkness and stars. Bright blue-rich light in the morning sets the body clock; amber light at night lets you sleep.

In the Crown the real sun comes through the slots, and the rooms' own lamps only fill in. In a dust storm, when the sky outside turns dark for weeks, the house keeps the same bright sols indoors.
